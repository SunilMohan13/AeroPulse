# AeroPulse — Hackathon Submission Video Script

**Runtime:** 3:05 · **Format:** 16:9, 1080p/4K · **Tool target:** Gemini (Veo) for generated shots + screen capture for product shots
**Geography:** Punjab – Haryana – Delhi NCR (Indo-Gangetic pollution corridor)

**How to use this file**

1. Each scene has three blocks: `VISUAL PROMPT` (paste into Gemini/Veo), `ON-SCREEN TEXT` (add in your editor), `VOICEOVER` (record or use TTS).
2. Veo generates ~8s clips. Scenes longer than 8s are split into shots `A`, `B`.
3. Scenes marked **[SCREEN CAPTURE]** should be real recordings of the AeroPulse UI/API, not generated video. Judges reward real product footage.
4. Keep the global style string (below) appended to every generated prompt so the film looks like one piece.

**Global style string — append to every Veo prompt:**

> `Cinematic documentary realism, shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm anamorphic, natural light, muted earth-and-amber palette with deep teal shadows, fine atmospheric haze, subtle handheld drift, shallow depth of field, no on-screen text, no watermarks, no logos, photorealistic, 24fps.`

**Voiceover direction:** Calm, measured, Indian English, documentary tone. Not hype. Slows down on numbers.

### Scene 9 · 1:56 – 2:14 · Detect and attribute  **[SCREEN CAPTURE of Event Detail + Evidence]**

**SCREEN CAPTURE direction**

> Record the event detail view: anomaly firing on the grid, source-likelihood bars, the evidence panel, then the lineage graph expanding from fire detections to the affected cells.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

```
Event evt_… — DETECTED → VALIDATING → CONFIRMED

Source likelihood        Evidence
  Biomass burning  HIGH    CPCB stations    6
  Regional transport MED    Fire detections 42
  Industry          LOW    Satellite       strong
  Traffic           LOW    Wind alignment  0.88
                           Citizen reports  3

Detection 94% · Source 82% · Forecast 89% · Overall 89%
```

**VOICEOVER:**

> An event is only opened when an anomaly, a minimum count of independent evidence, and a quality threshold are all met together. Then AeroPulse attributes it: biomass burning, high. Regional transport, medium. Industry and traffic, low. And it keeps detection, attribution, forecast and impact confidence separate — because they are different questions, and conflating them is how you get a confident wrong answer.

## Appendix B — Honesty guardrails for the submission

Do not claim on camera:

- that satellite feeds are live — Sentinel-5P, MODIS, INSAT and CAMS are **replay fixtures** in this build;
- that the air-quality values are ground truth — Open-Meteo is **CAMS-derived model output**, not CPCB measurement;
- that the anomaly, source-likelihood or forecast models are serving — they are at `VALIDATION`;
- that the shipped web UI is live-wired — it currently renders mock data.

Scenes 10 and 11 are shown as the designed capability, which is legitimate for a hackathon demo **provided** the narration stays in the present architectural tense as written and Scene 12 discloses the gate. If your hackathon requires a strict "what runs today" framing, add this one-line lower-third over Scene 12:

> `Live today: ingestion → grid → events → evidence → API, plus the gated PM2.5 estimator. Satellite feeds replay from fixtures; three models are held at VALIDATION by the gate.`
